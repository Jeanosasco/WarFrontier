# WarFrontier — Game Vision

## High concept

WarFrontier is a futuristic real-time strategy game about rebuilding civilization, engineering modular war machines, researching lost technologies, and fighting for control of hostile frontiers.

## Player fantasy

The player is a battlefield commander who designs armies, establishes forward bases, adapts technology to terrain and enemy behavior, and makes strategic decisions that affect the survival of entire factions.

## Core pillars

1. **Modular warfare** — vehicles and combat units are assembled from interchangeable bodies, propulsion systems, weapons, sensors, and support modules.
2. **Strategic construction** — bases are networks of production, research, defense, logistics, and energy rather than static collections of buildings.
3. **Meaningful research** — technology choices unlock distinct tactical identities instead of simple numerical upgrades.
4. **Persistent battlefield consequences** — terrain, wreckage, resource pressure, and mission outcomes influence later decisions.
5. **Original science-fiction identity** — WarFrontier will progressively replace inherited visual, narrative, audio, and gameplay content with original material.

## Setting

In the late twenty-second century, surviving human powers compete across regions known as Frontiers: dangerous territories containing abandoned infrastructure, rare resources, and remnants of unknown technology.

Each faction believes control of the Frontiers is necessary for humanity's future. Their conflict grows from border skirmishes into a global technological war.

## Initial playable experience

The first original scenario is provisionally titled **Operation First Frontier**. It will introduce:

- establishing a forward base;
- securing resources;
- designing and producing modular vehicles;
- reconnaissance with drones or sensor units;
- defending against an escalating attack;
- capturing a technological installation;
- choosing whether to extract, occupy, or destroy the objective.

## Gameplay loop

1. Scout the battlefield.
2. Secure resources and strategic positions.
3. Build logistics, production, research, and defenses.
4. Design units suited to the current threat.
5. Attack, defend, recover, and adapt.
6. Unlock technology and expand the operational frontier.

## Scope principles

- Preserve a working game while replacing systems incrementally.
- Prioritize one polished faction and mission before adding breadth.
- Avoid promising large-scale online services before the core simulation is stable.
- Keep all derivative code and distributions compliant with applicable licenses.

## Target audience

Players who enjoy classic base-building RTS games, technology trees, tactical unit design, science-fiction campaigns, skirmish AI, and community modding.

## Long-term direction

WarFrontier aims to become a modern, community-friendly RTS with original campaigns, factions, assets, maps, multiplayer modes, modding support, and transparent open-source development.
